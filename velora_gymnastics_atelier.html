The Art of Custom Tailoring

MADE TO REFLECT YOUR MOVEMENT.

“Every VELORA leotard is created with individuality in mind. From the first design idea to the final handcrafted detail, each piece is tailored to reflect the athlete's personality, style, and movement.”

  • I. Individual design Original artistic compositions conceived around your routine, music, and presence.
  • II. Tailored measurements Drafted exclusively to your exact anatomical proportions for second-skin security.
  • III. Handcrafted details Meticulous hand-appliqué, couture seam finishing, and precision crystal placement.
  • IV. Thoughtful material selection European stretch velvets, breathable Italian lycra, and translucent illusion meshes.
  • V. Personal communication Direct, attentive consultation with our atelier from initial sketch through final fitting.

Atelier Fitting Guide

How to Take Your Measurements

01. Height

Stand barefoot straight against a wall and measure from the crown of the head to the floor.

02. Chest Circumference

Measure horizontally around the fullest part of the chest with arms relaxed at the sides.

03. Waist Circumference

Measure around the narrowest natural waistline, keeping the tape snug without compressing.

04. Hip Circumference

Measure horizontally around the fullest part of the hips and seat.

05. Torso Girth (Essential)

Start at the center of one shoulder, pass the tape down the front through the legs, and return up the back to the starting shoulder point.
